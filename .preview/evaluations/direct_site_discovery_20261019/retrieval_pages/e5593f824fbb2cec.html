          Online insults in UAE can land you in jail, cost up to Dh500,000 fine

          As the UAE marks Cybersecurity Awareness Month, authorities are reminding users that what they post online carries real legal consequences

          • PUBLISHED: Mon 19 Oct 2026, 3:05 PM
          • By:
          • Amal Alduwaila AlHashmi
          Add as a preferred
          source on Google
          • Share:

          The UAE has warned social media users that insulting or defaming others online can result in imprisonment and fines of up to Dh500,000.

          The warning forms part of the nationwide 'The UAE is safe with your digital awareness" campaign, run by the UAE Ministry of Interior in cooperation with Dubai Police and the Dubai Electronic Security Centre (DESC) during Cybersecurity Awareness Month, observed each October.

          The campaign highlighted Article 43 of Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on Combating Rumours and Cybercrimes, which sets clear penalties for online insults and defamation.

          Stay up to date with the latest news. Follow KT on WhatsApp Channels.

          Under the law, anyone who insults another person or attributes to them an incident that could expose them to punishment or contempt through an information network or system may face imprisonment, a fine of between Dh250,000 and Dh500,000, or both.

          The penalty is aggravated when the target is a public official acting in an official capacity.

          Constructive, not harmful

          Beyond the legal warning, the campaign calls on users to treat social media as a platform for constructive expression and the respectful exchange of opinions, rather than personal attacks or mockery.

          It urges the public to promote positive dialogue and help build a safer digital environment that reflects the UAE's social values.

          A broader digital safety drive

          'The UAE is safe with your digital awareness' is a comprehensive public education initiative covering cybercrime, online fraud and the misuse of emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence. The campaign aims to strengthen digital literacy and encourage responsible online conduct across all segments of society.

          The campaign runs throughout October as part of the UAE's annual Cybersecurity Awareness Month observance.
